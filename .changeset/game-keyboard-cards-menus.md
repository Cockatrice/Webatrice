The game board works from the keyboard. Cards in the hand, on the battlefield, on the stack and in card views are focusable (arrow keys move and select, Shift+arrows extend, Ctrl+arrows move without selecting and Space marks or unmarks a card, Enter plays or taps, M moves cards to any zone and position, a lent card included, Shift+F10 opens the card menu, hold Z to zoom), and are read out with their state. Library, graveyard and exile piles are buttons named by their count and top card that open their menus, and each player block has a player menu button. An arrow or attach pick is announced and can be resolved with Enter on a card or a player's life. Tab stays Next Phase on the board; F6 leaves a zone. Every game menu now uses the shared accessible menu.
