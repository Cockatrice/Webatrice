Keyboard and screen-reader fixes on the game board. The game log is now read out as it happens: draws, moves, life changes and chat are announced once each, politely, and lines are not read again when the log drops its oldest one. Your life total is a spin button that says whose life it is and its value: Up and Down change it by one and Enter opens the set-life prompt. The mana pool is one tab stop, with Left and Right moving between colours and Up and Down adding or removing mana. Phase bar buttons are named even while the bar is collapsed, the current phase is marked, the bar expands while it has focus, and a player who may not change phases can still focus the buttons to read the current phase. Library, graveyard and other zone views take focus when they open (unless typing is kept in the game chat), name their search box and close button, close on Escape and give focus back. The game info, user details and "Put top cards on stack until…" dialogs now keep focus inside and return it on close. Each player in the player list has a "More actions" button that opens their menu from the keyboard, and the hand button opens the hand menu under itself from the keyboard; both menus support arrow keys, type-ahead and Escape. Game controls show a visible focus ring, and each seat is a landmark with its battlefield, stack and hand named by owner and card count.
