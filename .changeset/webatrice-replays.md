Replays. The Replays tab (top bar → Replays) lists the matches stored on the server for registered users, with desktop's actions: watch, download as a `.cor` file, save to local replays, toggle the expiration lock, delete, get a share code and look a replay up by share code. A local replay library kept in the browser holds folders and `.cor` files that you can import, open, rename, delete and save back out, and you can watch a picked `.cor` without importing it, also offline. Replays play on the regular game board in read-only mode, with a timeline that shows activity and seeks on click, play/pause, ±1 s and ±10 s skips, fast forward with a configurable speed, skipping of empty sections, and desktop's replay shortcuts.
