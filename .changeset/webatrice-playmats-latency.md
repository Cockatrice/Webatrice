Playmats and a ping display. On servers that support playmats (Servatrice 3.1), each player's chosen card art is shown behind their battlefield, cropped as on desktop, and your playmat is announced after you select a deck. A new Playmats settings tab mirrors desktop's playmat settings: who to show playmats for, whether your collection overrides a deck's own playmat, and the collection itself with its list mode and a crop editor. The top bar now shows the server round-trip time with a small graph; click it for the last, median, 95th-percentile and maximum ping.
