Deck share links and public decks on Servatrice 3.1 servers (Cockatrice #7241). My Decks can share a deck or a folder's decks by link, publish or unpublish a deck or folder (shown as Public or Public (inherited)), list and revoke your share links, and open a pasted link, Webatrice's or desktop's `cockatrice://opendeck`. The deck editor gains "Share deck...". A Webatrice share link (`#share=<token>&hostname=<host>&port=<port>`, kept in the fragment so the token never reaches the web host) shows the shared decks after login, read-only, with "Import to my decks". The user menu offers "View this user's public decks". None of it appears on a 3.0 server.
