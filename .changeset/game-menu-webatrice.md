Adds desktop's Game menu to the battlefield sidebar: Next phase, Next phase with action, Next turn, Reverse turn order, and Rotate view clockwise / counterclockwise. "Next phase with action" takes desktop's Shift+Tab, so the Webatrice-only "Previous phase" shortcut is now unbound by default (rebind it in Settings → Shortcuts if you used it). Reverse turn order and the two rotations are listed unbound, as on desktop. Also fixes "Transform into" missing on the first card of a game, P/T zone sorting leaving non-creatures in arbitrary order, and "reveal to all players" from the game-level reveal dialogs being rejected by the server.
