Settings is now a searchable page laid out like desktop's settings dialog, with Appearance, User Interface, Chat, Sound and Shortcuts sections whose changes are saved as you make them. It brings desktop's play-to-stack, tap animation and close-empty-card-view options; chat mentions, alert words and highlight colours; history, unregistered-user and private-message filters; in-game message macros; and sound with desktop's Default and Legacy sound themes, volume and a test button. Game events, mentions, private messages and buddies signing on now play desktop's sounds, and while Webatrice is in a background tab it can show system notifications (after you allow them in Settings) and marks the tab title; otherwise notifications appear inside the page. Existing settings and shortcut overrides carry over.
