Report users and work the moderation queue on Cockatrice 3.1 servers. "Report user" in every user context menu (user lists, chat names, the game player list) and on a player's page opens the report dialog (category, game, description and, when raised from a room or game chat, the recent chat log). "My Reports" (user menu and Account page) lists your reports with their status, comments and resolution, and pops up a notice when a moderator comments or resolves one. Moderators get a "Report Queue" with filters, assignment, resolve or dismiss with a note, comments, the reported user's history, statistics, joining the reported game as a spectator and opening its replay in the replay viewer. Nothing of this shows on 3.0 servers.
