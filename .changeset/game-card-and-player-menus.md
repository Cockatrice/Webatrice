More of desktop Cockatrice's in-game menus. Hand cards and library / sideboard view cards get desktop's card menu, including "Reveal to..." a player or everyone (plus an unbound "Reveal selected cards to all players" shortcut). Every card menu offers "View related cards", which shows the related card in the card-info pane. A read-only reveal window lets you hide cards from your view (Alt+H). "Open deck in deck editor" now opens the deck you are playing as an unsaved draft, whatever its origin. The player menu gains a Tally of the selected cards' subtypes or total power / toughness (shown over the board with the selection count), a Custom Zones submenu for servers that add zones, and a Say menu (Alt+1 … Alt+0) that sends the message macros you set up in Settings.
