The battlefield card menu, the battlefield's own menu and double-click, and the game's keyboard shortcuts now run the same card actions, and arrows and attachments go through one target pick, so the paths that had drifted apart now match desktop. "Draw arrow..." from a hand card plays the card and then draws the arrow from where it lands, as desktop does: onto the stack, or onto the battlefield for a land or when "Play all nonlands onto the stack" is off. A right-button drag can now start from a hand card, and an arrow pick can target a card in an open graveyard or exile view. An arrow to a card on the stack targets that card on the stack rather than the battlefield, an attach pick on a card off the battlefield cancels, as desktop refuses it, and a right-button drag keeps the colour of the modifier held when it started. Every way of playing a card, and dragging a card onto the battlefield from another zone, now lands it with its printed power and toughness; a played card that comes into play tapped arrives tapped. While an arrow or attach target pick is pending, a real card drag cancels the pick, and Escape is left to an open dialog.
