The board now follows desktop's board and card presentation settings. User Interface gains "Double-click cards to play them" (off plays on a single click), "Clicking plays all selected cards", "Do not delete arrows inside of subphases", "Auto focus search bar when card view window is opened", "Annotate card text on tokens", the drag-selection and total selection counts, "Keep game chat focused when clicking in game", and the animation settings: Enable all / Disable all, arrow draw animation, life counter flash and battlefield flash on damage, which follow the system's reduce-motion setting until you choose. With all four off, the board's other motion stops too (card flips, draw flights, the hand slide, the end step flash and hover transitions). Chat gains "Use game time instead of local time in game logs". Appearance gains the hand layout (a vertical hand, left justification), the minimum player count for a two-column table, card rendering (card names, auto-rotating sideways cards in the preview, hover scaling, rounded corners, the card font size in pixels), card layout (stack and vertical hand overlap, card view heights, which a header button or a title-bar double-click expands up to the zone's contents), card counter colours, the menus' shortcut hints, "Bump sets that the deck contains cards from to the top in the printing selector", and backgrounds for the hand, stack, table and player area, chosen from a card's art like a playmat. The board's selection, attach and doesn't-untap rings, its marquee, the text over card art and the mana pips now come from theme tokens; the attach and doesn't-untap rings darken in the light palette.
