Settings gains desktop's General and Storage sections, and Appearance gains a theme choice: it offers a Light, Dark or System palette that switches live and follows the operating system in System mode; existing users keep the dark palette. Every shipped translation (12 languages) can be chosen in General › Language or on the login page, and browsers reporting a regional language such as `pt-BR` now reach its catalogue. The Storage section shows browser storage usage and per-table counts, requests persistent storage, and clears the Scryfall cache or the imported card database without touching settings, shortcuts or servers. A "View debug log" dialog, reachable from the login page, the user menu and Settings › General, shows recent client messages with passwords, credentials and email addresses redacted, and can copy or clear them. The Account page's labels are now translated.
